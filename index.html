✍️ Phần V.b: Viết thư / Tự luận (~120 từ)

Tự luận

Đề bài:

Write a letter (about 120 words) to your friend/colleague/parents/relatives about a new friend that you met in your English class.


Your letter should include:

  • What does he/she look like? (Appearance)
  • What is his/her personality? (Qualities & character)
  • What things does he/she impress you with? (Talents, skills, actions)

⚠️ LƯU Ý: Write the body of the letter only. Do NOT write your name, your address and your signature in the letter!

Số từ hiện tại: 0 / 120 từ

SPEAKING CARD – No.1: Talk about your best friend

  • What is his / her name?
  • What is he/she like? (Appearance?)
  • What are his / her personality, quality and ability?
  • What does he/she like doing?
  • And say what he/she would like to do in the future.
